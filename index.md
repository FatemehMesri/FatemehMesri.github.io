# Fatemeh Mesri

**MSc Student in Physics ** | Computational Modelling | **Nonlinear Dynamics**

MSc student in Condensed Matter Physics at K. N. Toosi University of Technology (KNTU), with a BSc in Engineering Physics from the University of Tehran.
My academic interests lie at the intersection of theoretical physics, computational modelling, and complex systems, with a particular interest in applying mathematical and numerical methods to investigate physical and biological phenomena.

### Current Research
My MSc research focuses on computational modelling of neuronal network dynamics using the Izhikevich spiking-neuron model, with particular attention to synaptic plasticity and collective network behaviour.
